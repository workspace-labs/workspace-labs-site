Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

WorkSpace Labs builds software, and this website, workspace-labs.net, is ours. For anything about this policy or your data, write to hello@workspace-labs.net.

What this website collects

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Its fonts and pictures come from this domain, so reading it sends no request to anyone else.

Who else sees anything

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records the IP address of every visit for security purposes, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not receive those records.

If you write to us

A message to hello@workspace-labs.net reaches us through our domain’s mail forwarding and is kept in our mailbox. We use your address and what you wrote only to answer you, and we never add you to a mailing list.

Our apps

This policy covers the website. Before a WorkSpace Labs app is released, it gets its own section here, saying what it collects, what for, where it is kept and whether anything leaves your device.

Deleting your data

The only personal data we can hold is a message you sent us. To have it deleted, write to hello@workspace-labs.net from the same address and say so. We delete it and reply to confirm.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
